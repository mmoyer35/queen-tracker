🐝 Queen Tracker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Queen Tracker ("the app") is a personal record-keeping tool for beekeepers to track queen bees, hives, and inspections. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and how it is protected. We aim to collect as little as possible.

Who runs this app

Queen Tracker is operated by an individual beekeeper-developer. Questions or requests: mikemoyer35@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, used only to sign in and secure your data.
  • Your records: the queen, hive, inspection, treatment, and feeding information you enter.
  • Photos: pictures you choose to attach to a queen.
  • Voice notes: audio you record for a hive, plus the text transcript produced from it.

The app does not collect your location, contacts, or advertising identifiers, and contains no third-party advertising or tracking.

Where your data lives

Your data is stored in Supabase (our database and file storage provider). Every record is protected by row-level security, meaning you can only ever read or write your own data. Photos and audio are kept in private storage buckets scoped to your account.

Voice transcription

When you record a voice note, the audio is sent to a speech-to-text provider to transcribe it and then to structure it into an inspection, treatment, or feeding. We currently use Groq, with OpenAI as a fallback. Only that audio and its transcript are sent; no account or identity information is included. Both providers process API data to return the result and do not use it to train their models. See Groq's privacy policy and OpenAI's privacy policy.

Device permissions

  • Microphone — only while you are recording a voice note.
  • Camera — only to scan a hive's QR code or take a photo.
  • Photos — only to attach a picture you select.

Permissions are requested when first used, and you can revoke them anytime in your device settings.

Sharing

We do not sell your data or share it with anyone except the service providers needed to run the app (Supabase for storage, OpenAI for transcription), acting on our behalf.

Your control

You can export all your data as JSON or CSV from the app's menu at any time, and you can delete individual records. To delete your entire account and all associated data, email mikemoyer35@gmail.com and we will remove it.

Children

Queen Tracker is intended for adult beekeepers and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change and the new version will be posted here.
